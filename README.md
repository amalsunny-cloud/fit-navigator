Fit-Navigator

Fit-Naviagtor is a gym management system that enables gym owners, trainers, and members to interact with a user-friendly interface that supports role-based access.

Core Functionalities:

Here are the main features and functionalities of the system, categorized by user roles,

Admin Panel
a) Add, edit, or remove member details. b) Add trainers and assign them to members or groups. c) Create and customize membership plans (e.g., monthly, annual). d) Record payments and generate invoices. e)Track overdue or pending payments. f) View reports on attendance, and member growth.

Trainer Panel
a) Design individual or group workout plans. b) Assign plans to members based on fitness goals. c) Monitor member's performance. d) Record session attendance and notes. e) View and manage training schedules. f) Receive reminders for upcoming sessions.

Member Panel
a) View assigned workout plans and schedules. b) Check membership details and renewal dates. c) Track workout and diet progress with visual charts. d) View attendance. e) View payment receipts and upcoming due dates. f) Notify members of expiring memberships or upcoming sessions.

Additional feature: Accessible on both desktop and mobile devices.


Tech Stack Used: React, Vite, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, Razorpay, Bootstrap, Chart.js
